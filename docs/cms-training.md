# Using the CMS — a short course in five missions

## Before you start (30 seconds)

**Nothing here can break the site.** Every save is versioned and reversible — get lost, close
the tab, lose nothing.

**Save is not Publish.** If your change doesn't appear on the site, you saved a draft. That is
the single most common confusion, and the whole of section five.

**Do the missions in order.** Each is short and ends with something you can see.

---

## Mission 1 — Find your way around · 2 minutes

1. Go to **cms.magnifica.catholic.work/admin**
2. Look at the **left sidebar**. Six collections live there.
3. Click **Documents**.
4. Click **Magnifica Humanitas**.
5. Scroll the form. Change nothing.
6. Close the tab, or press back. **Do not press Save.**

**Done when:** you have seen the form and walked away without saving.

> Lost? The sidebar is always there. If it's hidden, reopen it from the icon at top left.

---

## Mission 2 — See the Spanish · 2 minutes

1. Sign in and find the **locale switch** in the top bar — it reads **EN** or **ES**.
2. Switch it to **ES**.
3. Sidebar → **Doc paragraphs**.
4. Open any paragraph.
5. Look at two fields: **extract** (the quoted sentence) and **note** (our commentary).

**Done when:** you are reading Spanish in both fields.

> The Spanish here is finished — all nine paragraphs have both. The essays do not.

---

## Mission 3 — Change something and watch it appear · 5 minutes

This mission teaches you the entire tool. It is worth the five minutes.

1. Sidebar → **Posts**.
2. Open **Introduction to Magnifica humanitas**.
3. Find the **abstract** field.
4. Add the word **TEST** at the end of it.
5. Press **Publish**. Not Save — **Publish**.
6. Wait two minutes.
7. Open **magnifica.catholic.work** and look at the essay.

**Done when:** you can see TEST on the live site.

> Nothing after three minutes? You pressed Save. Check the status reads **Published**.

---

## Mission 4 — Undo it · 1 minute

1. Back in the CMS, delete the word **TEST**.
2. Press **Publish**.
3. Wait two minutes and refresh the site.

**Done when:** TEST is gone.

**This is the real lesson.** You just changed the live internet and put it back.

---

## Mission 5 — Add a source · 3 minutes

1. Sidebar → **Sources** → **Create New**.
2. Fill in: **kind**, **authors**, **title**, **year**, **url**.
3. Set **verified_on** to today's date. It means *you checked the link works*.
4. Press **Publish**.

**Done when:** it appears in the Sources list.

---

## The three statuses — the genuinely confusing part

Every document is in one of three states, shown next to its title:

- **Draft** — never published. Nothing on the site at all.
- **Published** — live. This is what people see.
- **Changed** — live, **but you have newer edits the world cannot see yet.**

**If something looks out of date, you are in Changed.** Press Publish.

---

## Rescue card

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| My edit isn't on the site | You Saved instead of Published, or the status is **Changed** |
| I can't see the Spanish | The locale switch in the top bar is still on **EN** |
| I can't find a collection | The sidebar is collapsed — reopen it from the icon at top left |
| I'm overwhelmed | Close the tab. Nothing is lost. |

**You cannot break this.** Every version is kept, and nothing here cannot be undone.
